Chocolate Cake

Preparation Time: 45 minutes

Difficulty Level: Medium

Ingredients:

  • 1 cup flour
  • 1/2 cup cocoa powder
  • 1 cup sugar
  • 2 eggs
  • 1/2 cup milk
  • 1/2 cup butter
  • 1 tsp baking powder

Step-by-step Instructions:

  1. Preheat oven to 180°C.
  2. Mix flour, cocoa powder and baking powder.
  3. In another bowl, mix eggs, sugar and butter.
  4. Combine both mixtures and add milk.
  5. Pour batter into baking tray.
  6. Bake for 30 minutes.
  7. Let it cool before serving.
