Chicken Biryani

Preparation Time: 1 hour

Difficulty Level: Hard

Ingredients:

  • 500g chicken
  • 2 cups basmati rice
  • 2 onions (sliced)
  • 1 cup yogurt
  • Whole spices (cardamom, cloves)
  • 1 tbsp ginger-garlic paste
  • Salt and biryani masala

Step-by-step Instructions:

  1. Wash and soak rice for 20 minutes.
  2. Cook rice until 70% done and keep aside.
  3. Marinate chicken with yogurt and spices.
  4. Fry onions until golden brown.
  5. Cook chicken with spices until tender.
  6. Layer rice and chicken in a pot.
  7. Cover and cook on low flame for 15 minutes.
  8. Serve hot with raita.
