Vegetable Fried Rice

Preparation Time: 20 minutes

Difficulty Level: Easy

Ingredients:

  • 2 cups cooked rice
  • Mixed vegetables (carrot, beans, peas)
  • 2 tbsp oil
  • 2 cloves garlic
  • Soy sauce
  • Salt and pepper

Step-by-step Instructions:

  1. Heat oil in a pan.
  2. Add chopped garlic and sauté.
  3. Add vegetables and cook for few minutes.
  4. Add cooked rice.
  5. Add soy sauce, salt and pepper.
  6. Mix everything properly.
  7. Cook for 5 minutes and serve hot.
