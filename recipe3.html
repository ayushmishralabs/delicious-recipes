White Sauce Pasta

Preparation Time: 25 minutes

Difficulty Level: Easy

Ingredients:

  • 200g pasta
  • 2 tbsp butter
  • 2 tbsp flour
  • 2 cups milk
  • Vegetables (capsicum, corn)
  • Salt and pepper
  • Cheese

Step-by-step Instructions:

  1. Boil pasta and drain water.
  2. Heat butter and add flour.
  3. Slowly add milk and stir continuously.
  4. Add salt, pepper and cheese.
  5. Add vegetables and cook for few minutes.
  6. Mix pasta with sauce.
  7. Cook for 2-3 minutes.
  8. Serve hot.
